History, Vision & Transformation

About Smart City

Evolving from a historic regional trade and river-port hub into a modern sustainable metropolis, SMART CITY – Intelligent Living, Better Tomorrow integrates smart transit, clean solar grids, digital healthcare, e-governance, and citizen participation to deliver high quality of life and climate resilience.

Our Mission

Deliver transparent, inclusive, and technology-enabled civic services to every citizen.

Infrastructure Growth

4 Metro corridors, 1,240 km optical fiber, and smart flood-resilient drainage.

Digital Transformation

Unified Citizen ID wallet, online certificates, and real-time complaint tracking.

Future Projects

Phase-4 Metro expansion, Green Hydrogen transit pilot, and Urban Forest Ring.
